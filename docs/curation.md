# Scope and curation

[Collection](../README.md) · [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

Include research on generalist robot policies, cross-embodiment learning and shared autonomy, plus directly relevant methods, datasets, benchmarks and surveys. Exclude generic software-only agents (no robotics) and company announcements. Label resources without a confirmed paper as **Project**.

## Dates and ordering

Sort by year, newest first, then alphabetically by title. Use the first arXiv year when known; otherwise use the publication record. Keep one entry per work and distinguish conference years from proceedings years where needed.

## Summaries and evidence

Describe the contribution in plain language. 

Catalogue check statuses:

- `primary-record-checked`: matching bibliographic record.
- `primary-page-checked`: author, proceedings or institutional page.
- `primary-manuscript-checked`: accessible primary manuscript.
- `project-link-unresolved`: project URL could not be retrieved.
